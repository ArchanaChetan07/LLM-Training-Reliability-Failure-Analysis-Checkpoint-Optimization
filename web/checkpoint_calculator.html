Rates are from an adjudicated reading of Meta's OPT-175B logbook (992 A100s, 56 days, 50 unplanned interruptions), Shanghai AI Lab's Acme traces (NSDI '24) and the Llama 3 report. In Acme, total interruption rates barely scale with GPU count (β ≈ 0.1–0.4), so a per-GPU-hour rate extrapolates poorly beyond the scale it was measured at; hardware crash rates scale roughly linearly. Waste figures count lost work, checkpoint time and restart downtime as a share of wall-clock.
